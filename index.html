Common questions

Which course do I need?

Your site or employer usually sets this. Use the course finder or call us and we will point you to the right ticket.

Are your courses nationally recognised?

Our accredited courses are nationally recognised training. High risk work licence courses also lead to a licence from the state regulator.

Do I need a USI?

Yes. A Unique Student Identifier is needed for nationally recognised training, and it is free to create.

Can you train my team on site?

Yes. Tell us your location and team size and we will send a quote.

Not sure where to start?

Talk to our team and we will help you pick the right courses.
